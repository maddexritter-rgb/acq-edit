# Premiere cut-study mode

Use this mode to learn from completed native edits and make supported decisions
retrievable. Read the campaign master plan, current state and active case checkpoint
before work and after context loss. Campaign-specific paths and editor identity stay
in that project, never in this reusable skill.

## Start and resume

1. Read the campaign's accepted reference authority, publication permission, batch
   cadence, evaluation exclusions and source-acquisition limits.
2. Reconcile inventory by project hash, sequence UID and clip/revision family. A
   project, nest, alternate revision and distinct clip are different entities.
3. Verify the live brain read and authenticated publication route. Prove one real
   card through write, readback and retrieval before scaling. Keep at most the
   campaign's bounded unpublished backlog; do not substitute an accumulating archive.
4. Follow the shared Premiere capability guide. Inspect current project identity
   before target-dependent operations. Open working copies for relinking or export.
5. Resume the first incomplete durable step. Reconcile uncertain native operations
   and database transactions before retrying; never infer failure from a lost reply.

## One case, end to end

**Identify the reference.** Inspect the finished captioned sequence through the
Bridge, including its nested sequences, audible tracks and revision family. Record
why it is the finished edit using actual captions and completed picture treatment.
A FINAL name alone is insufficient. With accepted native authority, a delivered
MP4 is optional. Preserve ambiguous finished revisions without inventing an order.

**Read raw context first.** Locate the actual raw occurrences. Read the complete
interaction through its conclusion, including excluded branches and qualifications,
before reading old interpretations. Reuse checked transcripts and original media
identities. Review uncertain words or performance against relevant audio. Alignment
and matching text do not establish the correct occurrence by themselves.

**Reconstruct the spoken edit.** Save native item identities, original source sample
clocks, sequence ticks and rational frame rate. Flatten nested audio mappings without
counting picture changes as speech cuts. Track muted/disabled items and patches;
processed audio is an intermediate, not original recording evidence. Save the full
raw dialogue, final playback dialogue, ordered kept ranges, meaningful omissions,
reorders, overlaps/gaps and unresolved mappings. Read the joined final dialogue.

**Explain decisions.** For each useful choice, cite exact before/after words and
locations, the problem, observed choice, interpretation of its benefit, an alternative,
what must survive, applicability and exception. Record questionable choices and
counterexamples. Do not manufacture novelty: additional support or no new finding
is a valid disposition. Code reconstructs and checks; the model supplies reasoning.

**Publish and exercise.** Follow the packet/cut-map and publishing references. Search
duplicates by case, source and problem. Validate, publish, read back and test an actual
problem query plus an applicable second format and an inappropriate query. Record
what retrieved and why it is relevant; a top search result is not necessarily useful.
Verified native assembly evidence certifies only the packet's named spoken choices.
Acoustic uncertainty is allowed outside those choices; ambiguous wording, occurrence,
audibility or ordering within them is not.

## Knowledge transfer

Tag the observed format separately from applicable formats, with an explicit reason
for transfer. Index opening, repetition, context, causal logic, examples, order,
personality and payoff. A technique found in Q&A is not automatically Q&A-only or
universal. Conversation-dependent techniques keep their condition. Keep individual
cases in selective retrieval; do not grow mandatory reading with every case.

→ Use skill: `find-precedents` for live context and bounded problem retrieval.
→ Use skill: `assembly-design` and its evaluation reference for source-word tests.
The campaign may authorize independent baseline/treatment designers and a blind
reviewer. Use fresh contexts with pinned identical source and playbooks, mask treatment
labels, freeze literal selections and exclude evaluation source aliases. Separate
model preference, human acceptance and actual played-audio quality. Record failures.
General SOP promotion remains a proposal requiring the editor's approval.

## Durable campaign records

Use one master plan plus `campaign.json`, `state.json`, `registry.json`, case folders,
evaluation artifacts, reports and an append-only decision/event log. They are job
control and evidence, not another active knowledge store. Use `scripts/campaign.py`
to inventory projects and checkpoint existing case artifacts. Source hashes establish
identity, not finals or semantic verification. Registry entries start uninspected.

Checkpoint each durable step with evidence paths/hashes, next action and precise
blockers. Report at the campaign cadence, then continue without a human review wait.
Completed means published/retrievable, additional support recorded, or an explicit
no-new-finding result. A blocked case is counted separately. Track model tests and
costs honestly; absent cost data is unknown, not zero. Apply the campaign's bounded
recovery budget, move to eligible cases, and revisit when dependencies change.
