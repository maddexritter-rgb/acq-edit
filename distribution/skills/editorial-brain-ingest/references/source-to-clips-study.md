# Source-to-clips editor study

Use when the user provides full source footage, recordings or podcast links and
finished clips made from those sources, asking to learn the editor's method and add
it to the shared editorial brain. The outcome is reusable editorial judgment:
general principles with clear actions, supported by exact examples and exceptions.
It is not imitation of one editor's quirks or a requirement to reproduce every cut.

## Start and resume

Read the study's current plan, state, registry and first incomplete case after any
context loss. Record source/clip links, available files, editor attribution, scope,
accepted reference authority, exclusions and any requested review pause. Accept
links or local files; reuse verified assets and transcripts before acquiring more.
Ask only for information that cannot be recovered and actually blocks the work.

Use one versioned study directory in the existing project. For a new media project,
follow the user's project scaffold convention. Keep original assets and prior
analysis intact. A working study contains:

- a source/upload registry and upload-to-unique-cut reconciliation;
- source transcripts, reading records, occurrence mappings and per-case analyses;
- a frozen case manifest with hashes, synthesis, brain packets and readback receipts;
- any evaluation selections, comparisons, actual feedback and a resumable state log.

State tracks each case's evidence, completed step, unresolved claims and next action;
track publication and retrieval separately from analysis completion. Use immutable
revisions and append-only events. Existing `campaign.py` native project inventory is
for Premiere studies; do not represent it as a URL/public-clip inventory tool.

Inspect the current live brain and the configured authenticated publishing route
early. Missing publication access does not block local research: keep validated
packets, disclose `NOT_PUBLISHED`, and continue independent study work. Do not
rediscover an already configured connection or ask for secrets in chat. For a fresh
rebuild or masked evaluation, limit the early check to connection/policy metadata
and keep prior case interpretations outside the applicable freeze boundary.

## Reconcile the evidence

Deduplicate pasted URLs by canonical upload ID. Then compare actual spoken edits:
several uploads may share one cut, while similar titles may hide different edits.
Retain every upload alias, source identity, transcript version and path relocation.
Treat materially different spoken selections/order as separate cases. Report uploads,
unique cuts and unresolved groups with separate denominators.

Identify what was actually supplied: public finished asset, inspected captioned
native final, draft, or uncertain revision. An editor credit may be user-provided;
do not infer authorship or final authority from filenames alone. Route accepted
native evidence to [premiere-cut-study.md](premiere-cut-study.md) and
[cut-maps.md](cut-maps.md). Public-only evidence uses
[evidence-and-packets.md](evidence-and-packets.md)'s development research route.
The native references establish identity and mapping; the user's study plan and
this batch-synthesis mode govern the study's synthesis/publication cadence.

Read each full recording transcript to understand neighboring opportunities, then
read the complete relevant interaction through its conclusion **before** studying
its finished short. Include the question, rejected branches, qualifications and
neighboring opportunities. Audition source/reference audio for uncertain words,
speaker identity, repeated occurrences, conversational meaning or performance when
those affect a claim. State exactly what was heard; automated ASR or waveform
matching is not listening, and bounded auditions are not full-recording review.

Map the reference's actual spoken words to source passages in playback order. Pin
source and reference transcript identities/hashes, word IDs or clock ranges,
speakers, unmatched words, alternate occurrences, omissions and order changes.
Keep source clocks, reference clocks and native timeline clocks distinct. Verify
each claimed move against the raw occurrence and reference playback: high coverage
does not establish a correct reconstruction. Narrow or withhold unresolved claims;
do not fix an inconvenient transcript by inventing source words.

When rebuilding a criticized analysis, keep prior conclusions out of the new case
analysis until that analysis is frozen, unless the user directs otherwise. Save
the prior version and disclose unavoidable prior exposure. If the analytical
standard is new, contrasting calibration cases can help; pause only at a requested
review boundary, otherwise continue the authorized batch.

## Explain each cut as a sequence of decisions

Infer what the editor might have seen from observable choices. Separate **observed
selection/order**, **inferred benefit or intent**, and **our proposed improvement**.
Do not narrate private motives as established facts or assume every choice is good.
Every case answers these questions with literal words and evidence locators:

| Dimension | Required editorial explanation |
|---|---|
| Opportunity | What is valuable in the complete interaction? Which distinct viewer promises could it support? Why choose this one? |
| Opening | What do the exact first spoken words make a cold viewer expect? What context must survive? Compare another literal source opening. A title is not the spoken hook. |
| Progression | What does each retained beat add: understanding, mechanism, evidence, escalation, contrast, personality or emotional investment? Why here? |
| Omissions | Which substantive passages disappeared? What changes if each returns? Separate redundant meaning from material that could serve a different clip. |
| Restructuring | What moved relative to source order? What does the new order accomplish? Check referents, speakers, chronology, qualifications, causality and what a reply appears to answer. |
| Ending | How does the final line pay the opening's promise? Compare an earlier ending and later substantive source words; a memorable quote may precede the real payoff. |
| Transfer | What general editing decision does this illustrate, what evidence supports it, and when would the opposite choice be better? |

Compare plausible alternatives using the speaker's literal source words, not
rewritten connective copy. Read each proposed joined version. Preserve weak choices,
failed alternatives and counterexamples. An unpublished passage is available source
material, not proof that the editor considered and rejected it. Do not mistake
shorter runtime, more cuts or fewer disfluencies for a stronger editorial choice.

## Freeze the batch, then distill

Checkpoint case analyses as they finish, but synthesize cross-case principles after
all in-scope unique cuts are analyzed or an explicit partial scope is agreed. Freeze
the case manifest before consulting prior interpretations or making broad claims.
Do not publish a new general rule after every clip; preserve cases for later support
and contradiction. Source-first analysis and corpus synthesis are distinct steps.

Group decisions by the editor's problem, not the clip topic or an unusually narrow
story event. Ask what is shared across cases: creating a clear promise, choosing
an entry, earning an explanation or reversal, preserving progression, arranging
understanding, and completing a reward. These are organizing questions, not a fixed
number of findings or mandatory new rules.

For each proposed general principle provide:

1. **Principle:** a short, broadly understandable statement of the editing objective.
2. **Action:** observable steps a future session can take on its own source words.
3. **Check:** the literal alternatives or playback evidence used to judge the choice.
4. **Worked evidence:** supporting cases, contrary cases, scope and uncertainty.

For example, generalize an observer's changed expectation into keeping development
that earns a conclusion; use the particular reaction as a worked example beneath
that principle. Do not require future editors to encounter the same story scenario.
Keep useful one-off constructions as selective examples, with their applicability
and exceptions. Set no target number of lessons. A familiar principle with a better
example, additional support, a correction or no new finding is a valid result.

Compare each finding against current live playbooks, required reading and cards.
Classify it as already covered, better worked example, narrower exception,
contradiction, or meaningful addition. Preserve disagreement instead of blending it
into a vague maxim. Separate the recording's observed format from justified transfer
to other content units. Keep editor and brand identities in study evidence/profile
records; reusable skills hold procedure, not campaign-specific rules.

## Put the knowledge where future sessions receive it

Use the existing packet interfaces and validators in
[evidence-and-packets.md](evidence-and-packets.md), then follow
[supabase-publishing.md](supabase-publishing.md). Revisions preserve old evidence and
explain the change. Publish compact examples and conditional cards with citations,
actual compared words, an action, a check and an exception. Retain the frozen case
and mapping behind the card; do not upload full recordings/transcripts as retrieval
context. Make the evidence accessible to future sessions, not solely a local folder.

Stable skill code owns the procedure. The shared brain owns operational principles
and examples. Put a small reviewed general guide in the existing active playbook or
required-reading pack when authorized; detailed cases remain problem-retrieved.
Merely storing a source document does not make it required reading. Do not create a
second digest skill, copy the research library into skill prose, or make every case
mandatory reading. Preserved source SOPs do not override active playbooks.

Research publication and production activation are separate. Keep public-transcript
cards development and preserve `RAW_PUBLIC_FINAL_TRANSCRIPT_MATCH`; native timeline,
audible seams and caption verification require their own evidence. Normal production
retrieval excludes development cards. An authorized operating summary of existing
principles can be required reading without promoting its research examples or
claiming measured improvement. A new production rule follows the existing promotion
and user-authorization boundary; successful storage is not authorization to activate.

When the user asks to add findings to the brain, carry the authorized publication
through readback and retrieval; do not stop at local packets when the supported
writer is available. This does not authorize unrelated production-rule changes.

Read back exact source/card revisions, status, evidence class and body hashes. Test
problem-based retrieval with a relevant query, irrelevant query, exception case,
wrong format where applicable, normal production exclusion, and current/held-out
source aliases. Read what actually returns; relevance is not just a ranking count.
If required reading was authorized, fetch normal production preflight and confirm
the complete intended guide arrives with the active policy and correct hash.
→ Use skill: `find-precedents` for retrieval; do not duplicate its client or procedure.

Future assemblies apply or reject guidance against their actual source and record
consequential alternatives in the existing editorial intent/decision receipt.
Knowledge delivery, meaningful application, and quality of the resulting cut are
different results. A fetch receipt proves availability, not editorial judgment.

## Evaluate with paper cuts or assembled dialogue

Use the test the user asks for. For paper-cut comparison against the editor, give
the designer the full source but withhold reference clip contents, mappings and
analyses until the designer freezes its source-linked literal selections. Record
prior exposure; the same studied recording is a familiar-source development replay,
not an unseen test. Known reference titles can also leak moment selection.

→ Use skill: `assembly-design` for creating the test cuts. Freeze source/transcript
hashes, chosen moments, ordered source spans, literal playback, considered openings
and endings, and actual reading/card influence before revealing the references.
Do not claim a card changed a decision when it was only retrieved afterward.

After freezing, reconcile reference upload aliases and compare at distinct levels:
moment selection, promise, opening, retained development, omissions, order and payoff.
Separate same moment/similar construction, same moment/different construction, and
unpaired selections. Review the actual words, not just overlap scores. A reference
unpaired with a small pilot is not a demonstrated miss; an extra proposal is not
proof the editor rejected that moment. Do not force one-to-one matches.

Judge quality separately from similarity: promise clarity, sustained interest,
retained reasoning, earned reward and meaning preservation. Audible joins require
assembled audio and actual listening. Use neutral labels for preference comparison
when practical; disclose reviewer type and prior exposure. Attribute agent opinions
to the agent. Capture the human's actual words and exact reviewed revision/scope;
general praise is not per-pair preference, reasons, timeline approval or final approval.
Retain user-liked paper cuts as scoped worked examples when authorized, rather than
silently converting them to historical-final evidence.

For a baseline-versus-upgrade claim, freeze both designs on a separate recording
and exclude it and related evidence from research retrieval. Honor any user-selected
simpler test and report its limits. A paper replay, similarity result or successful
fetch alone does not prove the upgrade improves future finished clips. Extend an
existing canonical skill only when an observed procedural gap warrants it, with
specific authorization and backups of both protected roots before the change.

## Completion and handoff

Report what is analyzed, frozen, published, required reading, selectively retrievable,
development-only, and tested. Link the readable study and resumable state. List actual
gaps and the next eligible step; never collapse all stages into “saved to the brain.”
For a complete ingest, demonstrate write/readback/retrieval or identify the precise
`NOT_PUBLISHED` blocker. Research completion does not imply a proven editorial upgrade.
