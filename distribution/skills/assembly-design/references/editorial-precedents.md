## L1 research router

Start with the handoff index:

`__ACQ_EXTERNAL_VOLUME__/alex/2026-09-14_OliverQA/10_WORK/paired_cuts/evaluation/cut_design_handoff/index.json`

Load a case only when its decision problem matches the current interaction:

- correction/retraction: remove the wrong premise too, or retain the correction;
- question versus diagnosis: distinguish what was asked from what later facts revealed;
- sub-answer versus consultation: do not claim a whole resolution from one branch;
- condition/prerequisite: keep what makes advice applicable and in the correct order;
- attribution: preserve business, offer, channel, actor, metric, unit and certainty;
- reason-bearing reply: keep short owner speech when it changes the recommendation;
- reordering: verify referent, speaker knowledge and conversational function;
- operating-level transfer: individual advice does not automatically solve a team or
  owner-level promise;
- mechanism-bearing example: an example earns space when it supplies action, contrast,
  proof or completion rather than repeated color;
- performed ending or coda: inspect beyond the last token when picture, reaction or a
  late exchange completes or changes the payoff.

Label every applied precedent `FINAL_NATIVE`, `PARTIAL_NATIVE`, `TRANSCRIPT_ONLY`, or
`ACOUSTICALLY_UNVERIFIED`. A polished reference can contain a fidelity error. Full-source
meaning controls when a reference choice conflicts with correction, causality or scope.
