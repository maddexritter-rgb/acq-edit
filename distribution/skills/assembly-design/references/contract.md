# Assembly plan and handoff contract

Use `edit.assembly-plan/v1`. One pinned source clock per plan; multicamera sources map
onto it through the existing measured sync map. Do not combine independently timed
transcripts without a separately verified clock adapter. All word ranges are zero-based,
inclusive, and refer to the pinned JSON `words` array (`word,start,end,spk|speaker`).

During text development, save the same plan with `stage: "draft"`, explicit unresolved
issues and no invented `verification` package. Exact raw wording remains unchanged.
Drafts are reviewable but cannot compile. After actual verification and necessary
redesign, save a new revision with the real package and remove the draft flag.
For a branching interview, account for the complete selected exchange in
`interaction_words`; link later relevant qualifications in the coverage/context review
and widen the bounds when they affect this promise. Never shrink bounds to hide a branch.
Coverage may mark useful ideas `DEFERRED_OUTSIDE_BRIEF`; that is not an editorial rejection.

Required fields:

- `clip_id`, `intent`, `viewer`, `cleanup_review`: meaningful prose.
- `source_words`: absolute corrected word-file path; `source_sha256`: file SHA256.
- `interaction_words`: full candidate context bounds, including omitted branches.
- `segments`: playback-ordered `{words:[first,last],role,why}`; role is context,
  reasoning, example or reward. Backward moves require `reorder:{why,
  original_relationship,edited_relationship}`. Source words cannot repeat.
- `removals`: every unselected word inside the context bounds, grouped into ranges
  `{words,reason,why}`. Reasons use the existing L1 compiler's categories: off_arc,
  redundant, setup_detail, restart, filler, conversational_tail,
  replaced_by_clearer_statement. No percentage rule.
- `essential`: minimal source ranges `{words,role,why}`, including a reward. These
  anchors cannot replace reviewing the whole idea.
- `profiles`: nonempty subset of monologue, qa, l1_diagnosis, conversation_story.
- `editorial_review`: `promise`, `hook`, `payoff`, `context`, `qualifications`,
  `omitted_branches`, `reorder_safety`, `ending_continuation`, `alternatives` (prose).
  Quote exact source ranges for the hook, retention path and payoff in that prose;
  generic template language is invalid even when nonempty. Save actual alternative plans when comparing
  different arrangements. Record why the selected one wins, not only its label.
- `verification`: `{path,sha256}` identifying the package below.

Required sibling artifact `editorial_worksheet.json` uses
`assembly-design.editorial-worksheet/v1`. Read
[editorial-worksheet.md](editorial-worksheet.md). It pins the same corrected source and
complete `interaction_words`, contains the exact interaction and proposed assembly text,
accounts for every substantive branch, ranks the viable promises, and records the
question, reasoning ladder, payoff, omissions, reorders and cold read. The design gate
checks identity and structural completeness; it cannot author or certify the judgment.

After the spoken assembly passes the designer's self-check, jobs using
`edit.assembly-job/v2` or later also require before acoustic/native preparation:

- `performance_evidence`: a path/SHA256 pin to the analysis-only
  `cut.candidate-performance/v2` compatibility sidecar generated by
  `assembly-design/scripts/candidate_performance.py` for the interaction before selection.
  Its `words` cover every word of `interaction_words`; `events` flag measured gaps,
  explicit filler tokens, stretched words, and `vad_word_conflict` when ASR word
  boxes cover measured nonspeech despite having no lexical gap. It pins the same corrected words and
  reference audio as verification. It may be generated earlier only when a performance
  ambiguity materially affects the design. Frame numbers locate material; they are not
  certified edit boundaries. When reference audio is an excerpt,
  `audio_clock_offset_s` must come from the existing source mapping; never infer it
  from the first transcribed word. `PARTIAL` (for example, unavailable VAD) allows a
  editorial draft but not acoustic/native preparation.
- `performance_decisions`: `{event_id,action:keep|remove,why}` for every flagged
  event involving selected words. A kept gap requires the adjacent words to remain
  inside one selected segment; a removed gap requires a segment boundary. A selected
  filler, stretched word or VAD/word conflict requires an explicit keep rationale
  when it remains in a selected segment. A conflict near a cut edge requires source
  listening and corrected timing before acoustic approval; a decision entry alone
  does not make the ASR edge accurate. Omitted words are
  already accounted for in `removals`. These decisions are editorial; the map's
  flags never force a cut or certify that a stumble exists.

Required sibling artifact `design_comparison.json` uses
`assembly-design.design-comparison/v1`:

- `reviewed_plan:{path,sha256}` pins the exact `assembly_plan.json`.
- `interaction:{word_range:[first,last],text}` is the complete candidate interaction;
  its range must equal `interaction_words` and its text must be exact source words.
- `unedited_baseline:{word_ranges,text}` records the continuous raw interaction before
  editorial construction. It is not a title or summary.
- `selected_assembly:{word_ranges,text}` exactly matches `segments` in playback order.
- `hook`, `retention_path`, and `payoff` each contain exact selected `word_ranges`, an
  exact source `quote`, and a clip-specific `why`.
  Hook ranges must be a prefix of actual playback order; a buried line cannot certify
  an opening that still contains setup before it. This identity check cannot judge whether
  the quoted opening is compelling; independent review must assess that.
- `content_unit` contains nonempty, clip-specific judgments for `viewer`, `promise`,
  `reward`, `cold_viewer`, `goodwill`, `value_compression`, and `audience_growth`.
  These apply the canonical `audit-script/references/content_unit_review.md` rubric at
  design time. The exact hook, retention path, and payoff evidence above support these
  judgments; the prose cannot be a generic template reused across clips.
- `decisions` contains at least one source-indexed entry for each `scope` of `opening`,
  `body`, and `ending`. Each entry explains the actual keep/remove/move decision; generic
  phrases such as "trimmed for pacing" are insufficient.
- `alternative_review:{meaningful_alternative,why,...}` records the real competing
  arrangement and its ranges/text when one exists, or explains why a ceremonial second
  version would add no useful choice.
- `single_span` must agree with the plan. When true, `clean_source_justification` explains
  why the raw passage needs no internal editorial removal. This is evidence, not a waiver.

Run `scripts/design_quality_gate.py` on the batch before compile. The final editorial
gate runs the same checks again, so later acoustic or plan revisions cannot silently
collapse designed selections back into broad in/out windows. Exact duplicate editorial
rationale across different clips is treated as templated batch generation and blocks.

For any candidate including `l1_diagnosis`, also require `diagnosis` containing exactly
the existing v6 fields `source_coverage`, `promise`, `promise_ranking`, `format_review`,
`opening_review`, `ending_review`, `semantic_evidence`, and `branch_review`. Read
[`l1_cut_plan_schema.md`](../../edit/references/l1_cut_plan_schema.md) for their definitions.
Keep `start_boundary_review` / `end_boundary_review` on the corresponding segment when
that contract requires them. The assembly compiler passes these through the existing
v6 validator and retains its quoted evidence in the receipt. No second L1 implementation
or parallel plan is needed. Do not supply `diagnosis` without the matching profile.
Simple Q&A and monologues use the common contract; an evolving owner-specific diagnosis
inside a podcast or mixed workshop still requires this extension. Classification cannot
be certified mechanically: review it against the complete interaction. The ledger's
presence does not prove its completeness or the truth of its scope judgments.

Optional per-segment `performance`: `{source_seconds:[in,out], why,
evidence:{path,sha256}}`. Use only for a reviewed gesture, reaction or meaningful pause
attached to that segment's speech, in the same source clock. The evidence must identify
the reference media, clock mapping, observed action and review scope. Do not infer audio
review from a contact sheet. The compiler expands only that segment's cutter request
to encompass this interval, probes the pinned reference media duration and rejects
any extra words. Reconciliation requires the selected performance interval to survive
with its associated speech. Run it again on saved native ranges; a word-identical edit
that drops the selected reaction is not equivalent. No fake words, arbitrary silence
padding or independent word-empty shots. Separate nonverbal-only shots need a future
explicit interface; do not smuggle them into this one.

Pass compiled `speech_map_performance` as `speech_map_cut.py --performance '<JSON>'`
alongside the corresponding playback-ordered spans and `--preserve-opener`. The engine
also receives `--selection-spec CUT_REQUEST.json` with the pinned compiled word ranges;
do not let overlapping ASR times reselect the intended first/last words. The engine
searches for a verified nonspeech frame outside the protected endpoint, within its
existing search bound; no safe frame means REFUSED. After any merge/split, regenerate
this per-span mapping from the design rather than reusing misaligned entries. Reconcile
words and performance after resolution; a safe gap alone may still include unwanted speech.

Verification package (`edit.transcript-verification/v1`):

- `source_words`, `source_sha256` match the corrected source; `raw_words` and
  `raw_sha256` pin the immutable original ASR.
- `audio_path` identifies the actual reference audio/media; `audio_sha256` pins it.
  Performance intervals use this same clock; if it is an audio extraction, preserve
  evidence mapping it to the original picture in the performance review.
- `checked_words`: ranges covering the complete candidate region actually listened to.
- `reviewer`, `method`: actual identity and method, not an invented listening receipt.
- `unresolved`: array of unresolved issues; must be empty before compile.
- `corrections`: array of `{raw_words:[a,b], corrected_words:[c,d], heard,
  evidence:{path,sha256}, why}`; evidence is a saved listening note with source
  seconds or an audio/spectrogram artifact. Record insertions/deletions using encompassing
  neighboring ranges; retain raw/corrected files even if token counts differ. Never
  apply a text-only guess. A changed file requires a correction log.

The compiler validates pins and coverage, not whether the reviewer really heard the
audio or chose the right words. That remains an evidence-backed review responsibility.

Commands (use absolute paths):

```bash
python3 scripts/assembly_plan.py compile --job JOB.json --plan PLAN.json --out COMPILED.json
python3 scripts/assembly_plan.py reconcile --job JOB.json --plan PLAN.json --resolved RESOLVED.json --out RECONCILED.json
```

Compiled JSON includes existing `design`, `design_receipt`, `selection_guide`,
`assembled_text`, plus intended indices, source/plan hashes and `STRUCTURE_VALID`.
`assembled_dialogue` preserves source speaker labels and word ranges per turn/selection;
`assembled_dialogue_text` provides readable speaker-labeled dialogue. Missing speaker
labels remain `UNKNOWN`, never inferred from alternating lines or camera names.
`cut_request` is the retained `assembly-design/scripts/script_cut.py --spec` shape; save that object as
the spec without sorting, and pass `--preserve-internal`. It has one segment per
editorial selection, so intentional internal edits survive this mode.
It is input to Assembly Design's boundary helper, **not an import-ready timestamp spec**.

Resolved JSON: `{schema:"edit.assembly-resolved/v1", plan_sha256, source_sha256,
fps:"24000/1001", spans:[[in,out],...], acoustic_evidence:{path,sha256}}`.
Spans are actual source seconds in playback order on the rational source frame grid;
report paths must be real and pinned. `acoustic_evidence` points to the existing
speech-map JSON array; every span must have start/end `OK` or the engine's
strict `OK_MICRO_GAP` verdict and matching `result` times. A micro-gap is an
RMS-verified quiet frame between nonoverlapping word labels, not an active-word cut;
it still needs rendered-seam listening before native acceptance.
Save the invocation with the reference audio and transcript identities alongside it.
Adapt the existing cutter's report, do not author
a fake PASS. The reconciler checks full word containment and even partial overlap with
unwanted words, not just midpoint membership. Bad ASR timing can conservatively block;
repair timing against audio, version the words and recompile rather than ignoring it.

The existing speech-map report rounds seconds to three decimals. Recover its nearest
integer frame using the **same probed rational rate**, then save frame/rate seconds;
do not mistake rounded milliseconds for a different cut or use a rounded 23.98 rate.
Audit the recovered boundaries against the report and source audio. Refused boundaries,
ONSET_SNAP without a verified speech-free gap, or report/source ambiguity remain blocked.

Reconciliation returns `WORDS_RECONCILED`, assembly-clock words with original index,
speaker and source times, and source spans. Report verdicts/times are checked but their acoustic
judgment is **not** certified by this script. Review it and the rendered seams separately.
Every changed selection/timing/source invalidates downstream receipts and native review.
Output files are create-only; use revision-specific names to preserve prior evidence.

V4 jobs use one independent `edit.editorial-review/v1` response with perspective
`final_editorial`, defined in [`edit/references/assembly_job.md`](../../edit/references/assembly_job.md),
after acoustic resolution and reconciliation. It contains both the cold-viewer and
source-fidelity checks. V1-v3 retain their historical two-review contract. Preserve old
reports as historical evidence; they do not authorize a new revision.
`final_editorial_gate.py --job JOB.json --out NEW.json` validates the exact current
compile, final review and acoustic reconciliation.
It emits `EVIDENCE_VALID`, not a claim that code judged the clip compelling. Native
readback, actual seam listening and human acceptance remain separate gates.
