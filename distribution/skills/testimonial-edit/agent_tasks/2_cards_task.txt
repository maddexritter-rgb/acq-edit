Reverse-engineer the QUESTION CARDS for one L3 testimonial from the real interviewer questions.

Inputs in the WORKDIR I give you:
- clip_00.json  → {"words":[{word,start,end}]} full raw transcript (interviewer AND subject).
- cutlist_v2.json → {"spans":[{n,in,out,text}], ...} the KEPT answer spans (subject only; interviewer voice was cut).

The interviewer's actual questions live in the transcript GAPS before each kept answer span
(text with start-time between the previous span's out and this span's in). Extract them.

TASK: produce cards.json = a list of question cards to overlay before selected answers.
For each answer span that ANSWERS A DISTINCT INTERVIEWER QUESTION, create ONE card:
- Find the interviewer's real question in the gap right before that span (ignore the subject's
  trailing words from the previous answer and any "restate the question" chatter).
- Rewrite it TIGHT, 2nd person, ALL CAPS, as it would read on screen — like these reference cards:
  "HOW HAS YOUR BUSINESS BEEN SINCE YOUR LAST L3?"
  "WHAT WERE THE CHALLENGES YOU WERE FACING BEFORE YOU CAME TO L3?"
  "DID YOU HAVE DOUBTS AND FEARS AROUND SIGNING UP FOR L3?"
  "IF SOMEONE IN YOUR INDUSTRY IS ON THE FENCE ABOUT DOING L3... WHAT WOULD YOU TELL THEM?"
  When the real question matches one of those standard themes, prefer the reference wording.
- Split into 2-3 lines, each line <= ~30 characters, balanced.

RULES:
- One card per DISTINCT question. Do NOT card continuation spans of the same answer.
- Aim for 3-6 cards across the clip (the reference used ~4). Every clip MUST get at least 3
  cards (this is the whole point — no clip with zero cards). If the interview only has a few
  clean questions, card the strongest 3+.
- The FIRST span is usually the name/business intro or the opening answer — only card it if
  there's a real opening question ("how has business been since your last L3", "walk me
  through what shifted today"). The name/business lives in the super, not a card.
- Card the fence-sitter/advice question near the end if present.

OUTPUT: write cards.json to the WORKDIR:
{"cards":[{"span": <0-based index into cutlist spans>, "lines":["LINE ONE","LINE TWO"]}, ...]}
Spans must be valid indices, strictly increasing, no duplicates.

FINAL MESSAGE: one line — person, N cards, and the card texts.
