[VARIATION 1 — the overlay blend]
We select the two text layers and **create a compound clip. Then we go to video, blend mode, and

[VARIATION 2 — recolour the BACKGROUND only]
...if we want the background to become brighter or have a different colour, but we don't want the

[VARIATION 3 — text behind the head]

Now here's what we do. **We take the original image at the bottom of the timeline, we copy it and

## 🛑 WHAT THIS SETTLES, AND WHAT IT CHANGES

**1. The matte is CONFIRMED, and it is his method — not our inference.**
"Copy the image, place it above the text layers, remove the background" is exactly what our
`matte.py` (u2net_human_seg) reproduces. We reverse-engineered this from pixels in
VARIANT_matted_bigword.md; here he states it outright. No further verification needed.

**2. LEGIBILITY OUTRANKS THE EFFECT — and the fix is POSITION, not a weaker matte.**
His own rule: "the text still needs to be readable... it doesn't make sense to hide everything
completely behind the person." His remedy is to **move the text up, or move the subject down** —
he never suggests softening the knockout. So our `CINEMATIC_MATTE_STRENGTH` partial-knockout is
OUR invention, not his. It is a legitimate fallback, but the FIRST move should always be to place
the caption where the subject is narrow (which is already the measured rule in
VARIANT_matted_bigword.md, and now has the author's backing).

**3. The overlay blend is CONFIRMED, including the stacking trick.**
"Compound clip -> blend mode -> overlay", and duplicate the compound clip to brighten. Our
OVERLAY_PASSES is exactly his duplicate-to-brighten. He also states its PURPOSE plainly: **"the
text automatically adapts to the colour in the background."** It is a colour-adaptation device,
not a texture — which is why applying it to every word (as V9 did) reads as murk.

**4. NEW — recolour only the background, never the subject.**
Duplicate plate above the text, remove background, then grade the BOTTOM copy: the plate behind
the words darkens while the subject stays untouched. This is the clean fix for unreadable captions
on a busy plate, and it is strictly better than dimming the words.
🛑 NOT IMPLEMENTED AND NOT APPLIED — this is a colour change, and COLOUR GRADE IS MADDEX'S JOB.
Flag it, offer it, never switch it on unasked.

**5. Optical alignment, not just left-alignment.**
"Zoom in fully and look closely at the individual letters, then check where I can place the line
so that it fits perfectly over the other word." He aligns stacked lines BY EYE AT 100% ZOOM. Our
renderer left-aligns on a fixed x. That is close but not the same thing, and it is why his stacks
look tighter than ours.

**6. Carousels themselves: 3:4, AI-HD upscale every image, logo optional top-right/bottom-left.**
Not our pipeline today, but this is a complete recipe if ACQ ever wants quote carousels.
