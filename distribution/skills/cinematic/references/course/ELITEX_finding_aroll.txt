## WHAT THIS IS ACTUALLY TEACHING (the method, stripped of the examples)

His A-roll sourcing is REVERSE-ENGINEERING SOMEONE ELSE'S VIRAL CLIP back to its full source:

1. Start from a clip that already performed (not from a podcast you happen to have).
2. Identify the source. Two paths, in order of effort:
   a. The caption/handle names the guest + host -> search that title on YouTube.
   b. Nothing identifying -> **search the literal spoken sentence as the query.** The quote IS
      the search key.
3. Open the full (60-120 min) source video, open **Show transcript**, Ctrl-F the first few words
   of the hook. The transcript timestamp jumps you straight to the moment.
4. Judge the RESULT LIST BY QUALITY and keep scrolling until a high-quality upload appears —
   he explicitly rejects two low-quality results before taking a third. Resolution of the source
   is a selection criterion, not an afterthought.
5. Download that source (next lesson).

🛑 This is the same job our clip-miner does from the OTHER end: we already own the source and mine
it for moments. His constraint is that he does NOT own footage, so his whole pipeline starts from
proven-viral text and works backwards to a file. The transferable parts for us:
  - **Ctrl-F on the YouTube transcript is his seek tool.** Cheap, exact, no ASR needed when the
    source has captions.
  - **The spoken sentence is a better search key than any description of the clip.**
  - **Source resolution is a gate before anything else.** Reject and keep searching.
