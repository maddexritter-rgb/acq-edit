## RELEVANCE TO US — NOTHING TO ADOPT

This is the weakest lesson in the course for our purposes. It is a list of consumer GUI/web
downloaders aimed at editors with no terminal. **We already do this better:** `yt-dlp` handles
every one of these sources, scriptably, at native quality, and is what the rest of our pipeline
already uses.

The one substantive point that carries over is implicit in his A-roll lesson, not this one:
**he picks the highest-quality available upload and rejects low-quality results before
downloading.** With yt-dlp that is an explicit format selection rather than eyeballing a
results page — so our version of his rule is a format/height floor at download time.

🛑 Do NOT route any ACQ work through these third-party web downloaders. They re-encode, they
strip metadata, and they are an unnecessary external dependency for a job `yt-dlp` already does.
