## WHAT THIS IS ACTUALLY TEACHING

**Reverse-image search is the retrieval tool, Pinterest is the store.**

1. See a visual you want (in someone's reel, on a profile, anywhere) -> **screenshot it**.
2. Google Images -> upload the screenshot -> reverse search.
3. Scroll the results specifically hunting for **a Pinterest result** — that is the one that
   carries the clean, full-resolution original. Open it and download from there.
4. Fallbacks when Pinterest doesn't have it: Instagram, Twitter, plain Google.

**The feed is a long-term asset.** He deliberately trains the Pinterest recommender by repeatedly
searching cinematic/aesthetic, until the home feed becomes a curated visual library that needs no
searching. Sourcing cost drops to near zero over time.
