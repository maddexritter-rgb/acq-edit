# Instagram Reels text safe zone

Use this profile for a 9:16 Instagram Reel unless the delivery requirements supply a stricter one. Meta's Reels ads guidance says to keep key text clear of the interface, and its published ad specifications use 14% top, 35% bottom, and 6% on **each** side. Treat this as a conservative working boundary for organic Reels too; verify the final upload preview because app controls can change. Sources: [Meta Reels ads guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads), [Meta Reels ads guide PDF](https://d3m889aznlr23d.cloudfront.net/img/events/458925814/assets/e042d2be.reels_ads_guide1.pdf). The latter explicitly calls out the bottom 35% and links Meta's checker templates.

At 1080×1920, the allowed hook rectangle is **x=65..1015, y=269..1248** (950×979 px). Calculate from canvas dimensions using `ceil(.06*width)` on both sides, `ceil(.14*height)` at the top, and `floor(.65*height)` at the bottom. Apply a tighter supplied delivery rectangle by intersection. The transparent [guide image](instagram-reels-safe-zone-1080x1920.png) visualizes these boundaries; it is a guide layer and must not be burned into the finished video. A separately downloaded editor template can help visual review, but the numeric boundary in the renderer is authoritative.

Keep every fitted line box **centered on the canvas**, with equal left and right clearance. Check the *entire* painted hook including white or colored backgrounds, padding, and font glyphs; do not merely check the text anchor point. Do not move the hook left to evade right-side buttons. Reduce size only to the approved style's readability floor, try a faithful shorter headline or better line break, and then report a conflict if it still cannot fit.

Preserve the measured speech captions. Prefer the requested position directly below them with the 24 px-at-1080 gap. If the complete hook cannot fit there, try directly above the captions with the same gap, provided it is safe for the entire five-second display and does not cover a face or protected graphic. If neither centered position fits, stop; the editor must resolve the caption/framing/hook conflict explicitly. Never satisfy a safe-zone check by hiding part of the hook or moving it into Instagram's bottom or side interface areas.
